Program 01 · No fee to you

Buying direct from owners

Some sellers want the best possible price and should absolutely use an agent. Others want certainty and a date — a probate property, an inherited house in poor condition, a sale that has fallen through twice. We are the second option, and we will tell you plainly which you are.

Program 02 · Before the offer, not after

Survey & costing

Offer-then-renegotiate is the practice that gives cash buyers a bad name: a strong number agreed, then reduced a week before exchange once the seller is committed. We survey and cost first so the offer we make is the offer we complete at, barring something genuinely undisclosed.

  • Per property
  • Under offer
  • Our own cost
  • Access for inspection

Program 03 · Certified where required

Refurbishment

Money goes into roofs, damp, wiring and heating before anything a photograph shows. Gas and electrical work is done by registered trades and certified, and building regulations work is signed off — so the paperwork exists for the next owner rather than becoming their problem.
